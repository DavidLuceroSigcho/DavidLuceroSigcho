## Hello! I'm David 👋

I'm a **Full-Stack Developer and SaaS & AI builder** based in **Orellana, Ecuador**.

Through **[Methamonkey](https://methamonkey.com)**, I develop and operate interconnected products for real business needs, combining software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, databases, and cloud infrastructure.

My work includes frontend and backend development, APIs, hosting operations, multi-tenant business systems, and integrations. I enjoy building practical solutions and improving them through real-world deployment and testing.

- Creator and developer of the **Metha Ecosystem**.
- Focused on hospitality technology, business applications, AI agents, and self-hosted infrastructure.
- Participant in two **LaunchX Virtual Innovation hackathons powered by Microsoft**.
- Interested in collaboration on software products and digital transformation.

## ☁️ Building and operating the infrastructure

I work across different infrastructure roles so individual platforms can evolve independently:

```text
AI & AUTOMATION         Agents, n8n, messaging and system integrations
WEB & HOSTING           MethaPanel, MethaDB, WordPress, DNS and SSL/TLS
BUSINESS PLATFORMS      MethaPMS, CRM, ERP, Billing and Catalog development
MOBILE & TV             React Native / Expo apps and Android TV experiences
EMAIL SERVICES          Business mail infrastructure
```

**Engineering principle:** management tools should not become dependencies that can interrupt the applications they administer.
